=============================================================================
ALGORITHM: THE ONE-TIME PAD
INVENTORS: Frank Miller (Banker) & Gilbert Vernam (Engineer)
STATUS:    The Only "Perfect" Secret
=============================================================================

1. THE "NO PATTERN" RULE
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Imagine a book filled with completely random gibberish letters.
You have one copy. I have the other.
- I want to send "HELLO".
- I look at the first 5 random letters on page 1.
- I mix my word with that gibberish.
- result: Total noise.

2. WHY IT CAN'T BE CRACKED
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
If a spy intercepts the noise, they can't "solve" it.
Because the key is random, the noise could decode to literally anything.
"XJ9#M" could be "HELLO", "BOMB!", or "LUNCH".
Without the specific page of the book, every guess is equally right.

3. THE CATCH: HEAVY LIFTING
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
It has a 1-to-1 "cost."
- To send a 10-page letter, you must physically hand me a 10-page key first.
- To send a library, you must hand me a library.
- Once you use a page, you burn it. You can never reuse the "pad."
Perfect security, but totally useless for talking to a million soldiers.
