=============================================================================
ALGORITHM: HASHCASH (PROOF OF WORK)
INVENTOR:  Adam Back (British Cryptographer)
DATE:      1997
STATUS:    The Engine of Bitcoin
=============================================================================

1. THE ORIGINAL GOAL: STOPPING SPAM
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before Bitcoin, Adam Back invented this to fix email.
The problem: Sending email is free. A spammer can send 1 million emails for
zero cost.

The Solution: A "Digital Postage Stamp."
Adam Back wrote code that forced your computer to do a tiny bit of work
(about 1 second of math) before it could send an email.
- For you (sending 1 email): You don't even notice the 1 second.
- For a spammer (sending 1,000,000 emails): Their computer crashes. It would
  take months to calculate the stamps.

2. HOW IT WORKS (THE SUDOKU ANALOGY)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
How do you prove your computer did the work? You solve a puzzle.

Think of it like a Sudoku puzzle:
- SOLVING IT: Takes a human 30 minutes of hard brain work.
- CHECKING IT: Takes a friend 1 second to look at the grid and say "Correct."

Hashcash works the same way:
1. The Sender's computer must guess a random number millions of times until
   it gets a "Lucky Number" (a hash that starts with Zeros).
2. The Receiver's computer looks at the number and instantly knows you did
   the work.



3. SATOSHI'S GENIUS MOVE
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hashcash failed for email (people didn't like the delay).
But Satoshi Nakamoto saw it and realized it was perfect for money.

He put Hashcash into Bitcoin.
- Instead of "Sending Email," computers compete to "Build a Block."
- The "Work" requires huge amounts of electricity.
- This electricity cost is what secures the network. If a hacker wants to
  rewrite Bitcoin's history, they have to re-do all the electricity work
  from scratch. It is too expensive to cheat.
