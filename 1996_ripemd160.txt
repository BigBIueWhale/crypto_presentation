=============================================================================
ALGORITHM: RIPEMD-160 (RACE Integrity Primitives Evaluation Message Digest)
ORIGIN:    European Union (RACE Project) vs NSA
STATUS:    The "Shrink Ray"
=============================================================================

1. THE PARANOIA OF ORIGIN
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
In the 90s, almost all hash standards (MD5, SHA-1) came from the NSA.
Europeans didn't trust them. The academic community in Belgium created
RIPEMD as an open, transparent alternative.

Satoshi Nakamoto likely chose this not just for security, but for *size*.

2. THE MECHANISM (COMPRESSION)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
SHA-256 outputs 32 bytes. This is too long for a convenient "account number."
If you hashed a public key with only SHA-256, addresses would be unwieldy.

RIPEMD-160 crushes that 32-byte hash down to 20 bytes.

The "Hash160" Formula:
   Address_Hash = RIPEMD160( SHA256( Public_Key ) )

3. WHY USE TWO HASHES?
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
It shields the Public Key.
Until you spend your Bitcoin, your Public Key is not visible on the network;
only the "Hash160" (the address) is visible.

If a quantum computer breaks Elliptic Curve (ECDSA) tomorrow, it can derive
private keys from public keys. But it CANNOT derive a public key from a
RIPEMD hash.
* Unspent Bitcoin addresses are quantum-safe.
* Reused addresses are not.
