1. THE HARDCODED ANCHOR
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every block in Bitcoin points to the block before it. But what about the
very first one? It has no parent.

Block #0 cannot be mined like normal blocks. It is written directly into the
source code of the Bitcoin software. It is the hardcoded anchor that every
other computer agrees is the starting point.

If you download Bitcoin today, your computer still looks for this specific
block to know it is on the right network.

2. THE SECRET MESSAGE
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Satoshi left a note inside this block.
In the section of the transaction meant for arbitrary data (the "Coinbase"),
he typed a line of text instead of random numbers.

The Hex Data:

Decoded Text:
"The Times 03/Jan/2009 Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks"



3. WHY THIS MESSAGE MATTERS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
This sentence serves two powerful purposes:

A. Proof of Time (No Pre-Mine)
   By including a newspaper headline from that specific day, Satoshi proved
   he didn't secretly mine Bitcoin months in advance. He started the race
   fairly, on the day the newspaper was printed.

B. The Mission Statement
   The headline describes the failure of the old banking system (government
   bailouts). Placing it inside the first Bitcoin block forever sets the
   tone: Bitcoin was built as an escape hatch from that failing system.

4. THE UNSPENDABLE COINS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
The first 50 Bitcoins created in this block are weird. Due to a quirk in
the way Satoshi wrote the code, these 50 BTC can never be spent. They act
like a permanent trophy in the display case of the blockchain.
